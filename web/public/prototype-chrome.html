CHROME CARD — TEXTURE PROTOTYPE

The same Classic Chrome shelf card, drawn two ways. Left: the production SVG. Right: one WebGPU fragment shader, all materials procedural. Move your pointer over the shader — the studio light follows it.

What to look for: on the shader side the top plate's specular band and the lens glint follow the light; the leather pits shade directionally; the dial face has a conic sweep. The light idles in a slow sweep until you point at it. Known shader limits: the micro-text (aperture numerals, “125”, the OPEN FUJI engraving) is not drawn — text is the one thing fragment shaders do badly; in a real integration it stays as an SVG/DOM overlay on top of the shader materials. Everything else is the same geometry as the SVG, rebuilt from signed-distance shapes.
